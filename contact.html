Contact and Corrections

Why this publication has no monitored contact route and how to assess a factual correction.

Mira Voss · fictional editorial byline · analysis, not a product test

Angle & Edge does not provide an email inbox, contact form, telephone line, comment section, or reader-support service. The fictional byline on the articles is not a person who receives messages. No address shown elsewhere on the web should be assumed to represent this publication. Do not send a screen photograph containing private work, account credentials, a device serial number, legal notice, or medical information in the hope that this page is an intake channel. There is no monitored route here and no promise of a response.

What a correction would need to establish

A useful factual challenge to a fitting instruction would identify the page and passage, the specific model or attachment characteristic, and the evidence that contradicts it. For example, an official panel drawing might distinguish a visible image from the outside width of a glass cover; a manufacturer's mounting diagram might show that a tab cannot clear a camera. A sentence such as “14 inches is wrong” does not tell us which physical dimension is in dispute. Reproducible details matter more than a forceful conclusion.

Accessibility feedback similarly needs the affected task and setting, not a reader's diagnosis. “The focus indicator disappears behind the film at my normal brightness” is a more useful observation than a private medical history. Any illustration concern should identify the figure and the adjacent instruction it seems to contradict. These are standards for evaluating a correction, not instructions to transmit sensitive material to a nonexistent inbox.

No review desk or product intake

A product pitch cannot place a filter in these guides. This site has not accepted review units, performed controlled product comparisons, or assigned test scores. The external commerce article linked from the main feature belongs to another operator, whose contact options and policies are separate. A link here does not make that operator our support desk, nor does it grant Angle & Edge authority over its listings or purchasing process.

If a claim here turns out to be wrong, a responsible correction should identify the original statement, explain what changed, and distinguish a clarification from a factual reversal. There is currently no staffed correction workflow behind this page. Readers should independently verify model-specific details with official documentation before placing adhesive or other hardware on a delicate screen, particularly when an error could affect the camera, touch layer, or lid closure.

What you can do now

For a compatibility question, use the accessory maker's documented support or return process and your laptop maker's published specifications; do not send those parties confidential screen contents. For a device issued by an organization, use its approved IT, accessibility, or security channels when a work rule conflicts with a filter. Those are third-party routes for their respective responsibilities, not contact methods supplied or monitored by this site.

You can still use these pages without submitting anything. Keep your own notes on active-area dimensions, film dimensions, camera clearance, and the result of a benign dry fit. If a named product lacks enough information to answer those questions, defer the attachment rather than improvising a mount. At present there is no private data-submission facility on Angle & Edge; should a genuine contact route ever be added, it would require an explicit notice describing who operates it and what happens to messages.
