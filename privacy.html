Privacy Notes

What this static publication does not collect and what hosting infrastructure may process.

Mira Voss · fictional editorial byline · analysis, not a product test

Angle & Edge serves authored static pages, a stylesheet, a navigation script, and four local illustrations. No account registration or form submission is implemented in the site's own code. It does not set cookies, write to localStorage, call an analytics endpoint, embed advertising, or load third-party fonts. Opening the mobile menu changes its visibility in the current tab; that action is not sent by the script to a reader-profile service. These statements describe the files in this publication, not every piece of infrastructure between a reader and the site.

Requests still reach infrastructure

To deliver a page, the hosting provider and network intermediaries may receive a network address, requested path, time, user-agent or other request headers, and security or error diagnostics. We do not claim those operators retain no logs, know their exact retention schedules, or control their handling from this static HTML. The browser, operating system, network provider, and any organizational gateway may also maintain their own history. A lack of authored analytics does not make a visit anonymous.

The site makes no background request to the commerce article merely by displaying the main feature. It does request its own HTML, CSS, script, and image files as needed for rendering. The large hero image loads promptly; other images may load when approached in the viewport. Inspecting the source in a browser shows the authored requests, but it cannot by itself reveal every infrastructure log made while the files were delivered.

Leaving for a commerce article

The main feature has one outbound anchor to a separately operated LeStallion article. Following that link moves the reader to a different site. Its own cookies, logging, purchase process, disclosures, and terms may then apply; this privacy note does not speak for that operator. The anchor here does not add a tracking parameter, and the child guides and trust pages contain no commerce destination. We do not run a purchase flow or collect payment information on Angle & Edge.

An external link is not a private message channel. If a retailer or manufacturer asks for model details to answer a fit question, consider what information is necessary and use that organization's own documented privacy terms. A photograph of a blank test screen can often show a fit problem without revealing client names, documents, notifications, or login state.

No submissions or account data

There is no inbox, contact form, comment database, newsletter signup, upload endpoint, preference centre, or support-ticket system operated by these pages. The Contact page does not promise to receive corrections. We therefore do not claim to store reader-submitted messages or maintain a deletion portal for information that this site has never invited readers to submit. If functionality for submissions were added, the policy would need to explain who receives the data, how it is used, and how it is protected before collection begins.

These statements apply to the current authored static bundle. Changes to hosting configuration, embedded services, scripts, or contact capabilities could alter the data flow and require this notice to change. For sensitive work, rely on organizational device and network rules rather than on a broad privacy guarantee inferred from a static page. Do not use real protected documents merely to test whether a screen filter works from another seat.
